## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Venue parking information display
The system SHALL display parking instructions directing attendees to student parking lots at VWCC during the event.

#### Scenario: Parking instructions shown
- **WHEN** attendee views the 2026 event page
- **THEN** parking instructions SHALL indicate student parking lots (typically white on campus maps) are designated for event parking
- **AND** instructions SHALL direct attendees to follow signs to Hall Family Business/Science Building

### Requirement: Directions links availability
The system SHALL provide links to external directions and campus map resources.

#### Scenario: Directions link provided
- **WHEN** attendee views the 2026 event page
- **THEN** a link to directions to Virginia Western Community College SHALL be displayed

#### Scenario: Campus map link provided
- **WHEN** attendee views the 2026 event page
- **THEN** a link to VWCC Campus Map & Building Key (PDF) SHALL be displayed

### Requirement: Key venue locations display
The system SHALL display all key venue locations with building references and navigation guidance.

#### Scenario: Auditorium location shown
- **WHEN** attendee views the 2026 event page
- **THEN** the Auditorium location SHALL be identified as Hall Family Center for Business Science (also marked Whitman Theater)

#### Scenario: Registration location shown
- **WHEN** attendee views the 2026 event page
- **THEN** Registration location SHALL be identified as just outside Whitman Theater

#### Scenario: Vendor and cafe location shown
- **WHEN** attendee views the 2026 event page
- **THEN** Vendor and cafe area SHALL be identified as across the hall from the auditorium

#### Scenario: DefSec room location shown
- **WHEN** attendee views the 2026 event page
- **THEN** DefSec Room location SHALL be identified as Fralin Center with note that it is a short walk to an adjacent building around the courtyard

#### Scenario: CTF room location shown
- **WHEN** attendee views the 2026 event page
- **THEN** CTF Room location SHALL be identified as M302 in the Business Science Building

### Requirement: Campus navigation guidance
The system SHALL indicate that event signage will be posted to help attendees navigate.

#### Scenario: Navigation guidance provided
- **WHEN** attendee views the 2026 event page
- **THEN** a statement SHALL indicate everything will be marked from the auditorium
