Andrew is a devops engineer with eight years of experience.  He graduated from the University of Virginia in 2017 with a bachelor's degree in computer science and mathematics.  Currently, he is enrolled part-time at Virginia Tech in the pursuit of a master's degree in mathematics, focusing on applied algebra and its relationship to post-quantum cryptography.

Outside of software, he enjoys the outdoors --- growing up in Roanoke, Carvin's Cove and the Appalachian Trail provided many hours of enjoyment.
