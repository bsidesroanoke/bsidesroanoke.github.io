Part Red Teamer, part Community Leader, 100% Competitive. Regen Peterson is a Security Consultant at Rapid7 who carries enough certs to be dangerous and a passion for social impact as the Board President for Family Promise of Greater Roanoke. A staple of the Roanoke InfoSec community, he attends several local events and has even helped run the show at conferences such as Hack Red Con and Hack Space Con. When he’s not chaining vulnerabilities to create novel attack paths, he’s likely chaining combos in Super Smash Bros. Melee.
