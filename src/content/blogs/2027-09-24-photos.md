### 🔒 A Quick Note on Google Photos & Privacy

When opening the photo album on a computer or web browser, Google Photos may prompt you to **"Join"** the album.

**Joining the album will publicly display your Google account name and profile picture to anyone viewing the album.**

We recommend selecting _"Continue without joining"_ (or opening the link in a private/incognito browsing window).
