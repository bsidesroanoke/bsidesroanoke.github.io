You've probably heard of QEMU and that it can be used to emulate embedded systems for security research. In this session, I'll tell you about Renode and why I think it's a better solution than QEMU. I'll include a practical demonstration of how it can be used to fuzz, debug, and simulate the wireless networks of embedded systems to help you find more vulnerabilities faster.
